class  DSIBusSTM32
 IDSIBus backend for STM32 parts with a DSI host + LTDC (F469/ F479, F769/F779, H747/H757, U5x9, ...), built on STM32Cube HAL's HAL_DSI_* (command channel, adapted/video-mode DSI wrapper) and HAL_LTDC_* (the pixel-timing generator/framebuffer scanner DSI sits in front of on these parts - conceptually the same role ESP32-P4's DPI peripheral plays for DSIBusESP32).
